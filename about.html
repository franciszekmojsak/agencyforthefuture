who we are

A studio where design meets purpose and business

Agency for the Future is a Warsaw-based design studio operating within the Wise Habit ecosystem. We bring together designers, strategists and material specialists united by a single belief: thoughtful design can change the world.

With over 20 years of entrepreneurial perspective, we understand both the creative and commercial dimensions of every project. We design products that are beautiful, functional and responsible — from first concept to market-ready reality.

our values

What drives every decision we make

01

Empathy first

We design by deeply understanding clients, users, and all life forms affected by our work. Empathy is not a phase — it is our foundation.

02

Holistic thinking

From research and strategy through to production and market launch — we accompany each project across its full lifecycle.

03

Circular by default

Sustainability is not a feature we add at the end. We embed circular thinking into every stage of the design process from day one.

04

Diverse & inclusive

Our team brings together complementary disciplines and adaptive mindsets. Different perspectives produce better outcomes.
