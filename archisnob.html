about archisnob

A platform for architecture, design and sustainable living

Archisnob is the cultural and educational arm of Wise Habit — a platform dedicated to exploring the intersections of architecture, interior design, sustainable materials and contemporary design culture.

Through conferences, exhibitions, workshops and editorial content, Archisnob brings together architects, designers, material specialists and industry leaders to share knowledge and inspire better practice.

what we do

Events, exhibitions and knowledge sharing

01

Conferences

Annual gatherings bringing together practitioners, researchers and innovators to discuss the future of architecture and sustainable design.

02

Exhibitions

Curated material exhibitions at trade fairs and design weeks — including Architect@Work Warsaw and Prague — showcasing sustainable material innovations.

03

Workshops

Hands-on workshops for architects and designers exploring material qualities, circular design methods and sustainable specification processes.

04

Editorial

Longform content, interviews and case studies exploring meaningful design projects, innovative materials and emerging practices from across Europe.
