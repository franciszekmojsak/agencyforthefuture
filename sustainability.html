our approach

Sustainability is not a feature — it is the foundation

We believe sustainable design is not a trend or a checkbox. It is a responsibility embedded in every decision — from material selection and production methods to product longevity and end-of-life strategy.

Our team integrates circular design principles at every phase, ensuring that what we create contributes positively to both people and the planet. We work with clients to rethink, reduce and redesign.

sustainability pillars

How we embed sustainability into every project

01

Circular Design Strategies

We map the full product lifecycle and design for repairability, reuse, disassembly and recyclability from the very start of each project.

02

Sustainable Materials

Access to our Library of Sustainable Materials gives projects an advantage — over 1000 vetted samples from 40+ European brands chosen for environmental credentials.

03

Responsible Production

We work with a network of 350+ verified suppliers and manufacturers who meet our standards for environmental and social responsibility.

04

Impact Strategy

We help brands build and communicate their sustainability story in an authentic, measurable way — from product claims to certification pathways.

library of sustainable materials

A physical and digital resource in Warsaw

Our Library of Sustainable Materials is one of the most comprehensive collections of its kind in Central Europe. Located in Warsaw, it offers both physical samples and an online catalogue spanning bio-based, recycled, upcycled and low-impact materials.

The library is an open resource for designers, architects, brands and researchers seeking sustainable alternatives for their projects.
